The brand's all-caps display headline — use for every page and section title.

```jsx
<Headline heroL="UNVEIL">Unveil the best in you</Headline>
<Headline size="title" mix="BEAUTY">Express your inner beauty.</Headline>
```

- Pass `heroL` OR `mix`, never both, and only for 1–2 words.
- Titles are always uppercase, always left or right aligned (centre only for a short standalone line).
- Leading is 88–92% of the font size; tracking is 0.
