Sentence-case running copy. Subtitles sit at 20–40% of the title size; paragraphs at 50–65% of the subtitle.

```jsx
<Prose size="subtitle">Over 60 years of industry leadership and innovation</Prose>
<Prose>Lumenis develops life-changing, minimally invasive solutions…</Prose>
```

Never set body copy in all caps, and never in Arizona Mix.
