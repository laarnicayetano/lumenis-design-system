The brand CTA — all caps, squared, hairline border. Use for every call to action.

```jsx
<Button href="/products">Discover more</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Download kit</Button>
<Button variant="accent">Book a demo</Button>
```

- Copy is short and imperative: DISCOVER MORE, LEARN MORE, DOWNLOAD KIT, READ MORE.
- Leave clear space of one x-height above/below and 2x either side; keep at least one button-height between a headline and its CTA.
- `accent` variants pick up `--accent` from the surrounding `[data-subbrand]` scope.
