The black corporate footer with link columns, legal line, and social glyphs.

```jsx
<SiteFooter
  columns={[{ title: 'Aesthetics', links: ['Hair Removal', 'Body', 'Skin'] }]}
  social={['facebook-logo', 'instagram-logo', 'linkedin-logo']}
  policies={['Privacy Statement', 'Terms of Use']}
/>
```

Column headings are all caps; the links under them stay in sentence case.
